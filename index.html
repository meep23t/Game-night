How to play
  • Every card has an easy word worth 1 point and a hard word worth 2. Pick one and act it out before the timer runs out.
  • Tap Got it when someone guesses the word.
  • Tap Skip to pass a word. Once you pass it, it’s gone. It won’t come back this turn.
  • If you skip a word or time runs out, you get no points for that card.
How to play
  • Describe the word at the top so your group can guess it. Don’t say the word itself or any word under “Don’t say.”
  • No acting, pointing, or “sounds like.” Words only.
  • Tap Got it when someone guesses the word for 1 point.
  • Tap Buzz if you said a forbidden word, or Skip to pass. Either way the card is gone and scores no points.
  • One timer covers your whole turn, so get through as many cards as you can.

Word categories

How to play
  • Everyone secretly gets the same word, except the imposters. They only see “Imposter” (plus the category, if hints are on).
  • Pass the phone around. Each player taps their name to see their word, then taps the card to hide it. Forgot your word? Tap your name again before the clues start.
  • Starting with the first player, go around the circle. Each person says one word or a short clue about the secret word. Don’t make it too obvious, or the imposter will figure it out.
  • Go around once, then talk it over and vote on who you think the imposter is.
  • If the imposter gets caught, they get one guess at the secret word. If they guess it, they still win.

How to play
  • The first player gets a secret word and draws it.
  • Pass the phone. The next player looks at the drawing and writes their guess of what it is by typing it into the box. They only see the drawing, never the word.
  • The next player sees only that guess and draws it. Keep switching between drawing and guessing until everyone has had a turn.
  • At the end, flip through the sketchbook page by page and see how the word changed along the way.
  • With more than one sketchbook, each one starts with a different player, and the screen always says who’s next.

Level

How to play
  • Each turn, pass the phone to the clue giver. They see the two ends, like “cold” and “hot,” then swipe the dial to spin it and set a random hidden target. Only they see where it lands.
  • The clue giver says one clue that fits that spot. If the target is close to “hot,” you might say “soup.” Near the middle? Maybe “a sunny spring day.”
  • Pass the phone to the group. Talk it over and drag the dial to where you think the target is.
  • The closer you land, the more points: 4 for a bullseye, then 3, then 2. Everyone plays as one team and shares the score.
How to play
  • First, everyone fills the bowl: pass the phone around, tap your name, and secretly type in your words or short phrases. (Or switch off “Players write the words” and the game fills the bowl for you.)
  • Two teams take turns. On your turn, get your own team to guess as many words from the bowl as you can before time runs out.
  • Round 1, Describe it: say anything except the word. Round 2, One word: you can say only one word. Round 3, Act it out: no talking at all.
  • The same words come back every round, so pay attention! A round ends when the bowl is empty.
  • Tap Skip to put a word back in the bowl. Each word guessed is 1 point, and the team with the most points after 3 rounds wins.

Level

How to play
  • Put the phone in the middle. There are two teams, and each has a spymaster. Only spymasters may look at the secret key, which shows which words belong to each team.
  • On your team’s turn, your spymaster gives a one-word clue and a number, like “ocean, 2,” to link that many of your team’s words.
  • Your team talks it over, taps a word, then taps Reveal. Guess right and you can keep guessing, up to the number plus one. Hit a word that isn’t yours and your turn ends. You can also tap End turn.
  • Watch out for the trap card! Reveal it and your team loses right away.
  • The first team to find all its words wins.
