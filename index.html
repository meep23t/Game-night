Sound
How to play
  • Take turns acting while everyone else guesses. No talking!
  • Each turn has a few cards (set by “Cards per turn” under Rules). Every card has an easy word worth 1 point and a hard word worth 2. Pick one and act it out before that card’s timer runs out.
  • Tap Got it when someone guesses the word. Tap Skip to pass; a skipped word is gone for this turn.
  • Skip a card or run out of time and that card scores nothing.
  • Teams (needs at least 4 players): teams take turns acting, only your teammates guess, and points go to your team.
How to play
  • Take turns describing the word at the top so the others can guess it. Don’t say the word itself or any word under “Don’t say.”
  • No acting, pointing, or “sounds like.” Words only.
  • One timer covers your whole turn, so get through as many cards as you can. Tap Got it for each word guessed: 1 point.
  • Say a forbidden word and the card gets a Buzz: it’s gone, and you lose 1 point (unless “Lose a point for a buzz” is off under Rules). Tap Skip to pass on a card for free.
  • Who taps Buzz?
    • Playing as individuals: either the next person in line sits beside the describer, watches the screen and taps Buzz (they sit out guessing that turn), or play on the honor system and the describer buzzes themselves.
    • Playing as teams (needs at least 4 players): your teammates guess, and the other team watches the screen and taps Buzz. Points go to your team.

Word categories

How to play
  • Everyone secretly gets the same word, except the imposters. They only see “Imposter” (plus the category, if hints are on).
  • Pass the phone around. Each player taps their name to see their word, then taps Hide & pass the phone before handing it on.
  • Once everyone has seen their word, put the phone down. Going around once in the order shown, each person says one word or a short clue about the secret word. Don’t make it too obvious, or the imposter will figure it out!
  • Talk it over (there’s an optional discussion timer), then vote: on the count of three everyone points at a suspect, and one person taps + for each vote. A tie? The group picks who to go with.
  • Any imposter who gets away wins the round. If every imposter is caught, they get one guess at the secret word, out loud. Guess it and they still win; miss and the group wins.

Level

How to play
  • The first player gets a secret word and draws it.
  • Pass the phone. The next player looks at the drawing and writes their guess of what it is by typing it into the box. They only see the drawing, never the word.
  • The next player sees only that guess and draws it. Keep switching between drawing and guessing until everyone has had a turn.
  • At the end, flip through the sketchbook page by page and see how the word changed along the way.
  • With more than one sketchbook, each one starts with a different player, and the screen always says who’s next.
  • Scoring (“Score the sketchbooks” under Rules, on by default): after each sketchbook, the player who started it picks a favorite drawing and a favorite guess, worth 1 point each. If the last guess matches the word, the starter gets 1 point too. Most points wins.

How to play
  • Each turn, pass the phone to the clue giver. They see the two ends, like “cold” and “hot,” then swipe the dial to spin it and set a random hidden target. Only they see where it lands.
  • The clue giver says one clue that fits that spot. If the target is close to “hot,” you might say “soup.” Near the middle? Maybe “a sunny spring day.”
  • Pass the phone to the guessers. Talk it over and drag the dial to where you think the target is.
  • The closer you land, the more points: 4 for a bullseye, then 3, then 2.
  • Playing as individuals: everyone guesses together and shares one group score.
  • Teams (needs at least 4 players): teams take turns giving clues, only your teammates move the dial, and the points go to your team.
How to play
  • First, everyone fills the bowl: pass the phone around, tap your name, and secretly type in your words or short phrases. (Or switch off “Players write the words” and the game fills the bowl for you.)
  • There are three rounds with the same words, each with its own rule:
    • Round 1, Describe it: say anything except the word.
    • Round 2, One word: you can say only one word.
    • Round 3, Act it out: no talking at all.
  • On your turn, get the guessers to guess as many words from the bowl as you can before time runs out. Tap Skip to put a word back. A round ends when the bowl is empty, and the words come back next round, so pay attention!
  • 4 or more players: two teams take turns, and only your teammates guess. Each word guessed is 1 point for your team, and the team with the most points after 3 rounds wins.
  • 2–3 players: everyone plays for themselves. Take turns describing, and score a point for every word you get the others to guess. Most points after three rounds wins.

How to play
  • Put the phone in the middle. The board shows 16 words (25 on the big board).
  • 4 or more players: two teams, each with a spymaster. Pick the spymasters on the New board screen. Spymasters, sit next to each other so you can look at the key together; only they may look at the secret key.
  • On your team’s turn, your spymaster types a one-word clue and a number into the phone, like “ocean, 2,” to link that many of your team’s words. The clue can’t be a word on the board, and the number can’t be more than the words your team has left.
  • Your team talks it over, taps a word, then taps Reveal. Guess right and you can keep guessing, up to the number plus one. Hit a word that isn’t yours and your turn ends. You can also tap End turn.
  • Watch out for the trap card! Reveal it and your team loses right away (you can turn it off under Rules). The first team to find all its words wins.
  • Turn timer (optional, under Rules): when time runs out, the turn passes to the other team.
  • 2–3 players: it’s co-op. Everyone is on Team Blue with one spymaster. After each turn the clock takes a pink word. Find all your blue words before the pink ones run out.

                        How points work

                        1. Each game scores itself. While you play, every game keeps its own score with its usual rules, across all of its rounds.

                        2. The final result becomes night points. Only when a whole game is finished does its result turn into night points:

                        • Act it out, Describe it: final scores after all rounds. 1st place 3, 2nd 2, 3rd 1. Played as teams: like Word bowl.
                        • In the range: after all turns, each player’s tally (cluegiver 2, guessers 1 when the guess scores) is ranked. 1st 3, 2nd 2, 3rd 1. Played as teams: like Word bowl.
                        • Word bowl: after all three rounds, everyone on the winning team gets 2. A tie gives everyone 1. With 2–3 players: 1st 3, 2nd 2, 3rd 1.
                        • Word spies: each board is one game. Everyone on the winning team gets 2. Co-op with 2–3 players: a win gives everyone 2.
                        • Imposter: each round is one game. Everyone on the winning side gets 2.
                        • Pass the doodle: with scoring on, 1st 3, 2nd 2, 3rd 1. With scoring off, everyone gets 1.

                        3. Night points add up on the leaderboard. Ties share the place. Games you leave early, or mark “Don’t count”, add nothing.

                        Teams night (needs at least 2 players per team): only team scores are shown. Act it out, Describe it, In the range, Word bowl and Word spies are played team against team, and the winning team gets 2 points for each player on the smaller team (a tie splits it). In Imposter, Pass the doodle and small-group games, each team’s top scorers count, as many as the smaller team has, so uneven teams stay fair. Teams, team names and colors stay set all night. You can still sit someone out of a game, and someone new joins the smaller team.
